# 深蓝白手绘模切贴纸

![预览图](preview.webp)

## Prompt

```text
Transform the attached image into a minimal navy-and-white hand-drawn die-cut sticker illustration.

Treat the uploaded image as the sole source of content.

First identify the primary subject or subjects. Support:
- one person,
- one pet,
- two people,
- two pets,
- or one person with one pet.

Preserve the exact number and type of primary subjects.

Preserve:
- their left-to-right order,
- relative scale,
- interaction,
- pose,
- gesture,
- head angle,
- gaze direction,
- expression category,
- clothing categories,
- worn accessories,
- and any object essential to the action or story.

Preserve the source crop and viewpoint.

If the source is a close-up, keep it a close-up.

Never invent off-frame bodies, limbs, clothing, or scenery.

────────────────

【Character simplification】

Create a recognizable but deliberately simplified character interpretation, not a traced portrait.

Preserve identity through a small set of distinctive cues:
- face shape,
- hairstyle and silhouette,
- key accessories,
- characteristic expression,
- posture,
- and the relationship between subjects.

Do not reproduce:
- pores,
- wrinkles,
- individual eyelashes,
- realistic eye anatomy,
- or photographic skin detail.

For adults, use mild cartoon simplification only:
- slightly enlarged heads,
- moderately shortened torsos,
- simplified limbs,
- while clearly remaining adult.

Avoid:
- baby proportions,
- swollen cheeks,
- toddler bodies,
- oversized heads,
- or extreme chibi anatomy.

────────────────

【Illustration style】

Use clean, friendly editorial doodle art.

Use rounded, moderately thick, nearly uniform strokes with only slight hand-drawn variation.

Outer contours may be slightly stronger than interior lines.

Faces must be extremely economical:
- dot or short curved eyes,
- short brows,
- a one-stroke nose,
- and a simple mouth that retains the original expression.

The result should feel like a polished editorial doodle illustration rather than traced line art.

────────────────

【Color system】

Render all outlines and dark fills in one deep navy blue:

#173B7A

Never use pure black.

Use white as the default fill.

Convert clearly dark source areas, such as dark hair or dark clothing, into solid navy shapes.

Use only minimal white highlight cutouts when necessary for readability.

Keep human skin white and unshaded.

The default palette is strictly:
- deep navy,
- white,
- and a very pale neutral-gray shadow.

Do not introduce a third color unless the source contains at least two separate, clearly purple or lavender accessories or travel props that are narratively important.

Only in that case may those corresponding items use one flat muted lavender accent.

Otherwise:
- use no lavender,
- use no additional color.

────────────────

【Background removal】

Remove the entire original environment.

Remove:
- architecture,
- landscape,
- ordinary furniture,
- floor texture,
- incidental objects,
- unrelated people,
- signage,
- interface marks,
- logos,
- captions,
- and watermarks.

Place the simplified subject group alone on a clean, opaque white square canvas with generous margins.

────────────────

【Die-cut sticker treatment】

Wrap the complete subject group in one continuous wide white die-cut sticker border.

The border must surround the entire group as one cohesive sticker.

Do not create multiple separate stickers for multiple subjects.

Add a very subtle, soft, pale-gray shadow offset slightly downward and to the right.

The shadow should be just strong enough to make the white sticker border visible against the white canvas.

No transparency.

────────────────

【Composition】

Keep the original grouping and emotional relationship.

Compress only empty space.

Do not change the narrative.

Use a balanced, centered composition while preserving asymmetry when it comes from the source.

Include only the body parts visible in the source.

Retain the source framing.

If the source image is tightly cropped, preserve that crop.

If a limb, body part, clothing item, or environmental element is outside the original frame, do not invent it.

The finished image should feel like one cohesive sticker illustration.

────────────────

【Avoid】

Avoid:
- photorealism,
- line-art tracing,
- realistic portrait rendering,
- detailed anatomy,
- pencil texture,
- charcoal texture,
- watercolor,
- oil painting,
- anime,
- manga,
- extreme chibi style,
- 3D rendering,
- gradients,
- glow,
- paper texture,
- grain,
- halftone,
- collage,
- complex lighting,
- detailed backgrounds,
- added props,
- altered subject count,
- duplicated limbs,
- malformed hands,
- text,
- captions,
- logos,
- signatures,
- and watermarks.

Return exactly one finished square die-cut sticker illustration.
```
